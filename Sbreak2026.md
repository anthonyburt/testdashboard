### 🔑 Executive "Diva" Survival Tips
1. **The KSC Reveal:** At the Atlantis building, let the kids stand at the very front for the intro film. The "reveal" of the shuttle is the highlight of the trip.
2. **SeaWorld Dining:** Get the **All-Day Dining Deal**. It allows a meal/snack every 90 mins—perfect for keeping "diva" energy high without constant separate charges.
3. **Gold Status:** Confirm your **2:00 PM Late Checkout** at Titusville on Thursday morning. It allows the kids one last swim so they are tired for the long drive home.
4. **Buc-ee's Stop:** It is the world's cleanest pit stop. Get the "Beaver Nuggets" for the 7 and 9-year-olds; it's a Florida road trip rite of passage.
